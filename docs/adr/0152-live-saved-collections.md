# 0152. Followed and saved-search collections are live, read-only feeds whose new-post marks move only when the results are actually shown

Status: Accepted

## Context

Followed creators and saved searches show posts that were never saved. They are fetched from sources, may fail partly, and need "new post" marks that must not move because of a failed load, a refresh or a different sort.

## Decision

- Live feeds are session-owned and retained across navigation, including pagination and retry state (`SavedCodexSession`). `FollowedCodexViewModel` identifies its branches by follow membership and creator, so check or visit metadata never restarts an otherwise identical feed; creator pages are fetched and parsed off the main thread. A query collection owns live continuations, never durable Codex memberships.
- Live posts were never saved, so their post date stands in for save time. A live feed only appends: `LiveCodexFeedController` numbers each load (first page, load-more, retry) as a batch and publishes its posts in feed order, by batch, then by date inside it (oldest first under Oldest first, `updateSort`), so a later page never lands among posts already shown. A post another branch already showed keeps its place and the date it was shown with. Only the first load shows branches as they arrive; a load-more or retry publishes once every branch has answered. A refresh starts over at batch zero, except for posts of branches it did not replace. Explicit sorts (watched, length, creator, source) go through `sortCodexPosts` like a stored Codex, so they order the whole loaded list and keep feed order for ties. `LiveCodexFeedController` also provides stable branch order, bounded overlap and stale-result rejection.
- The Searches collection runs every saved search newest-first (`newestFirst`), whatever sort it was saved with, so its first page holds the posts the new-posts check counts; opening the saved search in Search keeps its saved sort.
- A refresh keeps the current pages until the new ones arrive. A branch whose refresh fails keeps its last good pages, shows its error, and the feed stays due for a reload; when every error is such a failed refresh of a branch that loaded at least once (`refreshFailedOnly`), the Saved shelf above those posts says it could not refresh rather than that posts failed to load; Retry runs the refresh again for the branches whose refresh failed only. A branch where only some providers failed shows their error and keeps the fresh pages the others returned; Retry there retries those providers. A reset or re-sort clears its related shelf because the anchor moves, and newly resolved durations refine the order quietly; live collections never promote shelf posts into themselves.
- `SavedSearchFeedLoader` is read-only: it never persists applied Search, Recents, statistics or new recommendation seeds.
- The app-open check (`SavedSearchNewPostsChecker`) runs the newest `MAX_CHECKED_SAVED_SEARCHES` searches, two at a time, newest-first whatever sort they were saved with (a random or popularity order shows older posts nothing uploaded). A search whose first page failed in any part contributes nothing, so a flaky source never counts its posts as new. Each search is stamped as attempted when its request starts (ADR 0019), so one cancelled by leaving the app waits out the interval rather than running again on every return.
- `SavedSearchOpenMarks` marks a saved search's new posts seen only once Search reports results for the same query without error; an offline or failed load, or a query changed meanwhile, leaves the marks alone, and posts the results left out stay new. Only the first such report counts.
- A new post stays new until it is seen: a visit to Followed or Searches marks a new post seen once at least half of its card's height lies inside the grid's viewport, or half the viewport for a card taller than it (`SeenPostsEffect`, read from the grid's layout info; a post counts only if it was new when its card crossed that line; seen posts collect while the grid scrolls and are written once it rests or the visit ends, so Followed's settings are not rewritten mid-scroll, and a write already started finishes even if the visit ends; one lost when the app dies only leaves a post New), and opening it in the Viewer marks it seen wherever it was opened. Posts the visit never scrolled to keep their marks, on the Saved shelves and on the next visit. The Saved shelves draw the same new-post markers on their cards and never mark anything seen themselves; a shelf on screen when a check finds new posts reloads its feed so their cards are there to mark (`rememberNewPostsReload` with `followNewMarks`), while an open collection reloads only once, when the visit starts, and remembers that with its back-stack entry, so neither a background check nor a trip to the Viewer moves the grid under the reader; either reload waits for a refresh already running instead of starting a second one. Inside a collection, the markers (an accent dot leading the top-right marks and an accent outline, announced as "New") come from a snapshot of the collection's new posts taken when the visit starts (`rememberNewPostMarkers`, held by the visit's back-stack entry so it survives a trip to the Viewer), so a card seen during the visit keeps its outline until the visit ends. A post the Viewer marks seen leaves that collection's markers at once through the session's `viewedNewPosts`, kept per collection because a post can be new in both. Markers only draw, so they never change which cards play. No other feed shows them. Marks keep their stored shape; clearing only ever removes keys, so no stored mark comes back and none a later check adds is dropped.
- The followed-creator filter sheet owns vertical scrolling, so author rows must not create a nested scroll surface (`FlowRow` there).

## Consequences

Marks reflect what the user was actually shown. A new live collection type needs its own branch identity that excludes volatile metadata.
