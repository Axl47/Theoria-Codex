# 0134. The For You owner keeps a stack of levels on one thread, rebuilds the Mix for deck inputs and only prunes it for hidden-tag changes

Status: Accepted

## Context

For You deals a Mix from the profile's lanes as a swipe deck or a grid, and each card can fork into a trail (a creator, Similar, a tag). Swipes must write to the profile in order, Undo must reverse exactly one decision, and a change to the hidden tags must not throw away the cards and Undo history the user is working through. The Viewer follows the top level while it is open.

## Decision

- `ForYouDeckViewModel` owns the Mix and its trails as a stack of `levels` (the Mix first, never empty), one deck each; the screen shows the top level as a deck or a grid over the same posts, and the Viewer follows that level as `ViewerStreamSource.FOR_YOU`. Level ids carry a per-owner `session` so an earlier owner's levels, whose Viewer may still be on the back stack, are told apart. Everything in the owner runs on the main thread, which is also where the decks report decisions. Steering lives in memory, so a recreated owner starts from a fresh Mix.
- `synchronize` starts a fresh Mix (`rebuild`: every level closes and steering starts over) when the profile, source, sort, media filter or set of runnable sources changes; a new profile also clears the session's decided posts. A change to the hidden tags only leaves out what they now hide (`dropHidden`: lanes and waiting posts go, a chip whose lane went widens to the Mix in place, and the levels, the deck and its Undo stay) and brings back what they no longer hide (`restoreLanes` plans the lanes again, adds the lanes the removed hides had kept out and keeps the levels; a hide that came back while the plan ran keeps its lanes out). The hides a Mix was planned under are remembered (`plannedUnder`) so only hides that kept lanes out trigger a replan.
- Mix levels are numbered (`sequence`) so a deck that a chip or a fresh Mix replaced no longer deals or steers; it only marks and unmarks its posts. Chips belong to the Mix; a trail keeps the one it was forked from. Selecting a chip replaces the Mix deck and the cards nobody decided are dealt again (`takeBack`).
- A post decided on any level, or a copy of it on another site (`copiesOf`, `FileMirrorIndex`), is never dealt again this session, and posts the profile already liked or saved (`kept`) are not dealt again. `isKnown` reads both sets live so testing one post copies neither. A trail leaves out what was decided, liked or saved before it opened and what is hidden, using a snapshot taken at open (`seen + kept`) because a card decided on the trail must stay in its deck for Undo.
- Every level's like, save, skip and hide is logged per profile as a verdict for a future taste model, and Undo removes it. Verdict writes run in `writeScope` under one mutex in the order asked, so an Undo never lands ahead of its swipe; the `ForYouDeckWiring.writeScope` outlives levels and the owner because the decks' writes must finish after either is gone. Each decision's verdict is kept by the decision itself (an identity map), so Undo removes that verdict and no later one, even when another deck decided the same post.
- Mix decisions steer (`DeckSteering`); a like or save also asks for up to `INJECTED` (2) posts like it (Similar, else the creator), registered in `asking` until it ends so Undo of the Like or Save can still stop it while its cards are vetted; they arrive a few cards later and are vetted like lane posts. Undo reverses the latest decision exactly (`lastDecision`); a write that failed later only loses the cards it asked for. Trails do not steer.
- Deals are `DECK_DEAL` (4) cards in the deck so what a like asks for lands a few cards later, `GRID_DEAL` (12, a screenful) in the grid, and small while lanes are still opening, so the lanes that answer next get their turn soon. The Mix deck asks for more with `DECK_PAGE_AHEAD` (2) cards left, trails with `TRAIL_PAGE_AHEAD` (5).
- Forking: a second tap while a trail resolves does nothing, and a trail that resolves after its level was left or covered is dropped. After a reconnect, a failed Mix rebuilds, lanes that `retriesOnReconnect` load again and a failed trail page retries.
- A level's feed, shared by the deck, the grid and the Viewer, is the source with the fuller copies of posts the Viewer resolved (a source whose listings carry no tags shows a hidden tag only once resolved) and without posts a hidden tag now hides; the deck takes the fuller copies unfiltered and passes over hidden posts itself so it keeps its place. `hiddenTokens` and the tagger's index are the one rule for all three. The grid hides nothing itself because the owner already applied For You's filters.
- Card forks (`forYouForks`) follow the Viewer's trail-row order: the creator when browsable (not on that creator's own trail, nor on a card that is here because of its creator), Similar when the source answers it, then up to `MAX_TAG_FORKS` (5) tags that neither the level's search nor the card's lane already covers; tags on nearly every post of the level (`feedShare`) are not offered. The fork row takes a sideways drag only while its forks overflow it; when they fit, the drag swipes the card.
- Every page of any lane or source teaches the file mirror index and records its posts as available, as Search's pages do (`ForYouCoordinator.loadPage`); post resolution is shared with the Viewer so a card and the Viewer asking for one post at once fetch it once. The posts a like asks for are fetched in `fetchContext`, off the main thread.
- Every level keeps its own grid place while the Viewer or a deeper trail covers it, a new level starts at the top, and a level that left the stack is forgotten for good (`ForgetRetainedFeedsEffect`, `LEVEL_ID_PREFIX`). Choosing a Codex for a card (picker or Create Codex) hands the Codex back to the deck, which saves it, so the card moves on and Undo covers it like any other save. A tag trail offers Save search; the Hidden tags row is not a filter, it opens the profile's list.

## Consequences

Changing the hidden tags is cheap and never loses Undo, but the owner carries several flags (`opening`, `sequence`, `plannedUnder`, `lastDecision`) that must stay consistent on one thread. Moving the owner off the main thread or sharing steering across sessions would need those made explicit.
