# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The search box also finds the series you follow, with their cover, manga or anime, and source. Picking one opens its page, also offline.
- Following says how many followed series need their extension, such as after restoring a Mihon backup, with a button to Extensions. Those series can't be checked for new chapters until their extension is on.
- Switch source starts from the series' title in a search field you can edit, so a series another source titles differently (English or romaji, other punctuation) can still be found.
- For You has a Refresh button in its top bar that deals a fresh Mix.
- When no sources are enabled, For You offers Manage sources, which opens Settings › Sites.
- Restore backup also takes backups from Mihon, TachiyomiJ2K and Anikku. Their followed manga and anime appear in Series, with the chapters you read, the episodes you watched, where you stopped in each, and each manga's reading mode. Nothing you already have is removed, and importing the same backup twice adds nothing more. The extension repos the backup lists are added too, without contacting them. The preview names the extensions still missing, so you only need to install them, and a series opens once its extension is on here. The next background check for new chapters notifies you of chapters and episodes the backup didn't have yet, unless you already refreshed that series.
- Backups Theoria makes include your extension repos. Restoring one adds the repos you don't have yet, so a new device finds the repos your series came from; which extensions are on stays on each device.
- Restoring a Mihon, TachiyomiJ2K or Anikku backup no longer lists an extension you have as missing when it, or its language, is only turned off; the preview names what to turn on in Extensions. An extension never turned on here still shows as missing.
- Picture tags now cover videos and Pixiv animations in your Library too. Each is tagged from its preview first, then, while the phone charges and sits unused (usually overnight), a few frames from across the clip refine its tags, so a scene the preview doesn't show still counts. If the frames can't be read, the preview's tags stay.
- Trails: in the Viewer, a row under the top bar shows the post's creator, Similar, and its most characteristic tags. Tapping one opens that stream in place, and you keep swiping from there. The steps you followed are listed beside it: tap one to return to it, or go Back one step at a time to the post you started from.
- Tapping a tag in the Viewer's information sheet starts a trail too. Holding the tag opens the Search moves (include, exclude, save).
- The Viewer's Save button shows the Codex the post most likely belongs in. Tap to save there, with Undo on the button for a few seconds. Hold it and slide onto another Codex, or New, to save there instead.
- Holding a post in any grid or shelf picks it up. Drop it on a Codex or New at the right edge to save it (with Undo), or on Like or Similar at the left. Letting go where you picked it up opens the post menu as before. Animated posts keep playing while you hold them, and in Search the Codices offered are the ones the Viewer's Save button would suggest.
- Triage: a one-post-at-a-time way through a feed, opened from Triage in the feed's filter sheet. Swipe right to like, left to skip, up to save to a suggested Codex, and down to hide one of the post's tags from For You. Undo reverses the last choice. The next card plays while you drag the top one away. If more posts fail to load, the deck offers Retry.
- The search box also finds your Codices, creators you follow or recently viewed, saved and recent searches, Settings pages, and a few switches you can flip right in the list.
- Codices found from the search box show their cover and how many posts they hold.
- Swiping the collapsed search bar sideways steps back and forward through earlier searches, each at the place you left it.
- When you open the app it checks followed creators and your most recently saved searches for new posts, at most every three hours. Library and its Saved section show a dot, and each row shows how many are new, until you open them. A saved search keeps its count until its results load, and posts its results leave out stay new.
- Galleries can be read left to right, right to left, or as one vertical scroll. Choose the mode at the top of a gallery's page overview; each gallery keeps its own choice, and the others follow Invert Scroll Direction. In vertical mode, swipe sideways to change post, pull down at the top to close, and the controls hide as you scroll down and return as you scroll up. The mode switch in the overview does the same as you scroll its pages. Vertical is offered for galleries of still images. Tall webtoon pages show sharp from the first page, the next six pages load ahead as you read, and with TalkBack the page in view offers Zoom and Media information.
- Search, creator profiles and For You show "No more results" at the bottom once a feed has no further pages, where the list used to just stop.
- A Codex's name is editable in place: tap it at the top of the Codex, type, and press Done.
- A Codex has a row of chips under its name. Filter by tag lists every tag in the Codex (type to narrow the list) and shows only posts that have all the tags you choose; the most common tags are one-tap shortcuts; the sort is a chip; Triage opens the deck. Search sites, in the tag list, searches the sites with the chosen tags.
- With the Manual sort in Library, hold a Codex and drag it to move it, with Undo. Letting go without moving it opens its menu as before.
- Triage on a Codex goes through its posts one at a time: swipe left to remove a post from the Codex, right to keep it. Undo reverses the last choice.
- Cleaning a Codex shows how many posts each comparison would remove before you confirm, and Review opens exactly those posts as a deck where left removes and right keeps.
- A Codex kept offline shows a ring on its tile and at the top of its page: how much is downloaded, and whether something failed. At the top of the Codex, tapping a full ring removes the offline copy (with Undo), tapping it during a download shows progress, and tapping an incomplete or failed one downloads the rest.
- While anything is downloading, a pill at the top of Library shows what's left. It opens one sheet listing offline Codices and downloads.
- Closing the app with Back no longer cancels your downloads: files already downloading finish, and the rest of the queue continues when you come back.
- Saving a post into a Codex kept offline downloads that post too.
- History: swipe a search or a watched post left to remove just that entry, with Undo in its place. Holding a search opens a menu with Save search and Remove from History; a post's hold menu has Remove from History too.
- History: the bookmark on a search saves it at once under its query, and Rename edits the name right in the row. A saved search shows a filled bookmark.
- Creator pages show the creator's most common tags as chips. Tapping one opens a trail for that tag.
- On a creator you follow, a divider marks where the posts you've already seen begin.
- For You is a deck: one post at a time, dealt in turn from your interests. Swipe right to like, left to skip, up to save to a suggested Codex, and down to hide one of the post's tags; Undo reverses the last choice. A label under the card names the interest that dealt it.
- For You: chips above the deck, Mix and one per interest, narrow the deck to that interest.
- For You: buttons under the card open more of one of its tags, its creator, or similar posts as a deck of their own. The steps you followed are listed at the top: tap one to return to it, or go Back one step at a time. A tag's deck has Save search.
- For You: liking or saving a card brings two similar posts a few cards later, labelled "Like one you liked" (or "More by" its creator where the site has no similar posts), and skipping several cards from one interest in a row deals that interest less often until you leave.
- For You: the grid button shows the same posts as a grid, and the deck button returns. The grid has no buttons under its posts: a post's tags, creator and similar posts open from the deck. The app remembers which one you used.
- For You: Hidden tags, in For You's Filters, lists the tags you've hidden and lets you unhide them.
- For You: with more than one profile, a chip at the top switches between them.
- The search box finds every setting: switches flip right in the list, and the rest open where they live.
- Followed creators: Check for new posts shows that it is running, and each creator has a Following button with Undo in the row.
- Series: a new tab for manga from Mihon extensions. Browse a source's popular series, search every source at once, and open a series to see its chapters.
- Extensions are third-party code, so each one stays off until you turn it on under Extensions, in the Series tab or in Settings. Turning one on shows the key it is signed with. The same page chooses an extension's languages and holds its settings.
- A source in the Series tab can show its latest series as well as its popular ones, where the source offers them. Filter searches just that source, with its own filters (genres, status, sort and so on); the chip shows how many you changed. Choices last until you pick another source or leave the tab.
- Chapters open in the Viewer, read left to right, right to left, or as one vertical scroll, with the mode kept per series. A chapter resumes at the page you left, is marked read when you finish it, and continues into the next one.
- Follow a series to keep it on the Series tab with its unread count. Followed series are checked for new chapters when you pull down on the tab and in the background (Settings, Check for new chapters), and a notification for a new chapter opens the series.
- Extension sources behind a browser check, such as Cloudflare's, get through on their own when the site allows it. When the site needs you to pass it yourself, the source says it needs browser verification, and Verify in browser opens the site over the screen you're on; once Theoria gets through, the page closes and the source loads again.
- When a source falls behind or breaks, Switch source in a series' ⋮ menu finds the same series on your other sources and moves it there. Follow and reading mode carry over. Each chapter keeps its read state and the page you stopped at, and one you left unread stays unread; chapters the old source didn't have count as read when they come before the one you were reading. The chapters already out are not announced as new. Open shows the other source's page first without moving anything.
- When an extension is on in several languages, the Series tab and series pages name the language next to the source.
- Profile backups include the series you follow, started, or marked chapters read in, with each chapter's read state and last page and the series' reading mode. Restoring only adds: nothing already on the device is undone. Which extensions are turned on is never in a backup, so a restored series says "Extension unavailable" until its extension is installed and turned on.
- Series chapters can be downloaded to read with no connection, even with the extension turned off. Tap the mark on a chapter to download it, cancel it while it downloads, or delete it once it's on the device. The Download menu above the chapters downloads the next 5 unread, all unread or all chapters, or deletes a series' downloads. Downloads continue in the background with a notification, also after a restart, and a chapter that can't be downloaded shows as not downloaded again.
- A followed series can download its new chapters by itself (Download new chapters, in the same menu), on Wi-Fi and other unmetered networks only.
- In Settings, below Check for new chapters: Delete after reading removes the downloaded chapters you've read when you close the reader.
- Downloaded chapters are listed under Offline in the Library's sheet, one row per series, and count toward offline storage. Removing a series' row deletes its chapters and cancels the ones waiting to download; Remove all offline copies does both for every series.
- The app now reads the list of installed apps to find extensions, and asks to send notifications when you follow a series.
- Series: anime from Aniyomi extensions, beside manga. Turn an anime extension on under Extensions, then browse, search and follow anime in the same Series tab; anime covers carry a play mark, and All, Manga and Anime chips narrow the tab once you have both.
- Extensions install from the same repos Mihon and Aniyomi use. Add a repo under Extensions, then Get extensions lists what it offers in your device's languages, or anything by name. Installing one turns it on. Theoria keeps its own copy, so it stays when you uninstall Mihon, Aniyomi or their forks.
- The Extensions page lists updates your repos offer, with Update all. Updating an extension another app installed gives Theoria its own copy and keeps it on or off as it was.
- An extension installed from a repo has Remove on its page.
- A repo that starts signing with a different key than the one it had when you added it offers nothing until you add it again.
- Episodes play in the Viewer. An episode resumes where you stopped, even past the point where it counts as watched (85%), and starts over only if you left it in its last two minutes. It goes on to the next one when it ends.
- While an episode plays, the stream button lists every server and quality the source found, with subtitles and audio beside it and next episode on the right. Double-tap a side of the picture to skip 10 seconds.
- Subtitles come on by themselves, in your phone's language when the episode has it. Off stays off for the episodes that follow.
- When a stream doesn't start, the next one is tried for you; when one stops partway because its link expired, a fresh link is fetched and playback picks up where it was. Retry fetches fresh links too.
- New episodes of an anime you follow are checked and notified like new chapters.
- A notification for one new chapter or episode has a Read or Watch button that opens it in the Viewer; tapping the notification still opens the series.
- Switch source works for anime too, between anime sources, and keeps the episodes you've watched and where you stopped in each. An episode the old source didn't have stays unwatched.
- Extensions that stream by torrent are listed but can't be turned on, and an anime its source splits into seasons says seasons aren't supported yet. Episodes can't be downloaded yet.
- Following has Sort & filter: show All, Unread, Not started, In progress, Caught up or Completed series, and sort by Most unread, Last updated, Recently read, Recently added or Title. Your choice is kept, a dot on the button shows while a filter hides series, and No matches offers Show all.
- Statistics count time spent reading series and watching anime as their own categories, Reading and Anime, also in shared stats and comparisons.
- Sites: every built-in site has a Re-test button, as added sites do. A built-in site's page shows its address with Working and Re-test, so a site that needs no account no longer opens to a blank page.
- Extensions: with nothing installed and no repo yet, the page offers Add repo. Once you have a repo, Repos leads on to Get extensions.
- Get extensions can show other languages: pick one, or All languages, from the language chip. A search looks through the languages chosen there (your device's, by default), and offers matches in other languages when it finds none. With both manga and anime on offer, All, Manga and Anime narrow the list to one kind.

### Changed

- For You no longer deals cards you skipped in the last 30 days, even after the app restarts. Start over at the end of the Mix brings them back.
- For You opens on the cards that were next when you left, instead of a blank deck while it loads. New cards follow behind them, so nothing moves under your finger; Refresh deals a fresh Mix.
- The app opens sooner when you have many downloaded chapters or offline copies: "Preparing your library…" no longer waits while every download is checked. For a moment after opening, the Offline sheet shows its progress bar until that check finishes.
- Opening a post in the Viewer loads its first page before looking up the posts next to it, so the first page appears sooner on a slow connection.
- For You learns from every tag of the posts you like, not only the first 20 in the site's order. On Gelbooru and Danbooru, whose tags come alphabetically, interests further down the alphabet now shape your Mix too, including for posts you liked before this update. A post you both liked and saved counts once.
- For You plans a Mix faster when you have liked many posts.
- Still images in the Viewer fill the screen's width in portrait, without the gap at the top or the border around each image: manga chapters, galleries and single pictures alike. Videos and animations keep their place below the top bar.
- Viewer subtitles now render as outlined white text with no dark box behind them (unless Android's system caption style is turned on) and sit closer to the bottom of the picture when controls are hidden, moving clear of the timeline when controls appear.
- Episode playback in the Viewer now shows the series and episode on two lines at the top left, puts Subtitles, Audio, Servers and More (with Start over) at the top right, centers Previous, Play/Pause and Next over the picture, and places Lock, Rotate, Speed, Picture-in-picture and landscape Fit/Fill cleanly above the timeline. Server, subtitle, audio and speed pickers now open as spacious bottom sheets.
- Locking an episode hides controls and blocks accidental taps, swipes, pinches and Back; tapping or pressing Back reveals only the lock icon to unlock, and lock stays active across automatic moves to the next episode.
- Video timelines across the Viewer now tint the buffered range ahead of the current playback position.
- Pulling down on the Series tab notifies you of the new chapters and episodes it finds, the same way the background check does.
- The background check for new chapters checks quiet series less often. A series that releases often is still checked every time; one with nothing new for months is checked every two days. The check also waits while the battery is low. Pulling down on Following still checks every series.
- Downloading chapters, checking followed series for new chapters, a series' images and a chapter reopened from where you left off start only the extension that series comes from, not every extension that is on, so they no longer wait for the others to load.
- In the reader's chapter strip, downloaded chapters and chapters the reader has loaded show their first page instead of the series cover, so chapters are easier to tell apart.
- When a series' chapters open on the same page, such as a scanlation group's credits, the reader's chapter strip shows each chapter's second page instead, and remembers that for the series.
- Chapter pictures in the reader's strip show the top of the page, below its blank margin, so a long webtoon page no longer shows a slice from its middle.
- When a series chapter's pages fail to load, the Diagnostics failure log records why, including whether the site refused the request outright.
- The Diagnostics failure log names the extension source behind a failed image, and records when an extension image was sent without its source's own connection.
- The Diagnostics failure log records why an extension failed to load, and when one needed something Theoria doesn't provide.
- A tag trail now narrows the search you were in, adding the tag to it, instead of searching that tag alone.
- In the Viewer's information sheet, tags the current search already covers show as included or excluded.
- Animated only now asks each site for animated posts instead of filtering a mixed page on the phone, so animated results fill the feed from the first page, on Pixiv especially. Turning it on or off re-runs the search like changing the sort, and saved and recent searches remember it. If you had it on, tap it once more after updating.
- The composer's built-in "Animated" saved tag group is gone: Animated only now does that job everywhere.
- A tag trail keeps the filters of the level it starts from: Animated only, the date range, the sort, excluded tags, and hiding liked, saved or watched posts.
- Trail chips lead with tags that are clearly more common in what you like and save than in what you watch, such as the characters, series and artists you follow, plus your saved tags, saved searches and repeated searches, then add tags too rare to judge yet. Tags that are on everything alike, such as "image", "blush" or "censored", tags on nearly every post in the current stream, and tags for text on the image, such as "artist name", "watermark" or "twitter username", are left out.
- Triage no longer offers to hide tags that are on nearly everything you watch.
- The Save button and the Save to Codex list suggest the right Codex more often. A tag that is rare in your library counts for more than one that is on half of it, and a Codex you just saved into moves up for the next half hour or so.
- The app has three tabs: Search, For You and Library. Library holds Codices, Saved (Likes, Followed, Searches) and History, which was Recents. Settings opens from the gear at the top of Library.
- The Viewer's actions (Like, Info, playback settings, page overview, More) moved from the top bar to a dock at the bottom of the screen.
- Search runs as you edit it: adding, removing or excluding a tag, or changing the source, sort or dates, updates the results after a short pause. The Search button is gone: the keyboard's Done key adds the tag you typed, and pressing it on an empty field closes the editor.
- The search editor is a sheet over the results instead of a full screen, so you can watch the results change.
- A search is added to History once you close the editor on it, scroll its results, or open a post, so half-built searches no longer appear there.
- Post menus in For You and on creator pages now show tag counts, and holding a tag saves it, as they already did in Search, the Codex and the Viewer.
- A Codex collection's "Resolving durations…" line now spans the full width, as in other feeds.
- In a post's hold menu, the creator's name at the top now opens their profile, as in the Viewer's information sheet, replacing the separate creator button.
- Removing no longer asks first where it can be undone. Removing posts from a Codex, deleting a Codex, and removing a custom site, a download or an offline copy happen at once, with Undo where the item was. Nothing is actually deleted until Undo is gone: a deleted Codex comes back until you leave Library, and files stay until the sheet or screen is left or the Undo runs out.
- Follow on a creator page is a labelled button, and unfollowing shows Undo in its place.
- Hiding a tag in For You now hides every interest that includes it, and every post that carries it, not only interests with exactly the same tags. Hidden tags lists them.
- For You shelves you pinned, and For You searches you saved, are now ordinary saved searches that open in Search: one per site the shelf searched, named for the site when there are several.
- Swiping sideways on For You's deck no longer switches tabs, so it cannot fight a card swipe: change tabs there from the bottom bar. The other tabs, and For You's grid, swipe between tabs as before.
- Downloads started from For You are labelled "For You" instead of "FYP".
- Opening For You no longer adds an entry to History, and earlier For You entries are gone from it.
- Settings keeps what you set once. Hidden tags moved to For You, Source mix to Search's source picker (with Unified chosen), and default video quality to the Viewer: the quality you pick in a video's playback settings is used for other videos too. Offline Codices and downloads are managed from Library.
- Custom site edits apply as you make them, without a Save button. Keys still have Save.
- Backup has its own page in Settings. Back up profiles opens the file picker directly, with a switch for including History and statistics.
- The app says "posts" where it sometimes said "items", and "Codices" where backups said "collections". Empty lists show a short label instead of a sentence.
- Every site that wants a browser check, built-in, added in Sites or from an extension, now asks the same way: Verify in browser opens the site over the screen you're on instead of sending you to Settings, and closes by itself once Theoria gets through, so the screen you were on loads again. If the site lets the browser in but still refuses Theoria, the page says so. A built-in site you've verified once renews its check out of sight when it expires, as added sites and extensions do.
- Series cards now show their title inside the bottom of the cover over a scrim, mark already-followed series with a Following badge in browse and search, and the Series search bar matches the Search tab's collapsing pill bar.
- In global Series search, each source header opens that source's dedicated search when tapped, and shows its language underneath when your sources' languages differ.
- The Series tab has two views, Following and Browse, switched at the top. It opens on Following once you follow something, and on Browse until then.
- Browse shows one source at a time: tap its name to pick another source, grouped into Manga and Anime, then choose Popular or Latest and Filter beside it. A source without Latest shows only Filter. The same controls sit above a source's own search.
- The search bar narrows Following by title as you type; on Browse it searches every source. When nothing you follow matches, Search all sources takes the title to Browse.
- Series pages now open with a blurred cover backdrop, a Follow button beside Open in browser and Share, tap-to-zoom cover preview, long-press title copy, an expandable About section with genre tags that open that source's genre filter, a full-width Continue reading button, pull-to-refresh, card-styled chapters showing scanlator groups, and a floating top bar that hides as you scroll down and returns as you scroll up.
- On a series page, the top bar now searches chapters inline, holding a chapter offers per-chapter read/unread, Mark previous as read, and download actions, bulk read/unread changes offer Undo, and the chapter list's filter button remembers sort and filter choices per series with quick-clear pills under the header.
- Back up profiles opens a sheet holding Include History and statistics and the Back up button. The Backup page itself shows Back up profiles and Restore backup, and notes that Restore also takes Mihon and Aniyomi backups. Daily backup has a group of its own below them.
- A looping clip in the Viewer lets the screen sleep after 10 minutes without a touch once it has started over, like reading a chapter. Episodes and videos that play once still keep it on while they play.
- Statistics stop growing with every tag you see: each site keeps counts for its most-watched tags. A niche tag kept on only two or three posts may stop counting as an interest, so Trails offer it less. A backup with statistics made once a site reaches that limit needs this version or newer to restore.

### Removed

- For You's shelves, with See all, pins and dismissals, and its video length filter. The deck and its grid replace them.
- The "For You searches" count in Statistics.

### Fixed

- Saved searches and Followed show their new posts first when you open them after the count says there are some. They used to show the posts from your last visit, and the new ones only turned up while scrolling, some jumping to the top.
- A series you followed before its source listed any chapters now notifies you when its first ones come out, when the source dates its chapters. Before, those first chapters arrived silently.
- Pressing Back twice quickly to leave the Viewer now goes back two screens. The second Back used to be lost while the post slid back into its card.
- Download new chapters also gets the chapters a series page finds when you open or refresh it. Before, opening the page counted them as seen, so they never downloaded.
- A turned-off or removed extension no longer holds up other chapter downloads: chapters from other sources keep downloading, and a notification says how many chapters wait for their extension.
- A series notification opens its series even when the app was last opened from a notification for the same series. It used to only bring the app forward.
- When chapter downloads fail within one series, the notification opens that series, and names the chapter when only one failed.
- Pulling down on Following says when followed series couldn't be checked, with Retry, instead of looking the same as finding nothing new.
- The Series tab no longer loads a source's listing while it shows Following or a search.
- A series page's chapter list stays smoother while chapters download.
- In the Viewer's page overview, the Back arrow at the top closes the overview instead of the whole Viewer, the trail row no longer covers the reading-mode switch, and the last pages are no longer hidden behind the buttons at the bottom.
- The screen no longer turns off while you read a manga chapter. After 10 minutes without a touch or page turn it sleeps as usual; slideshows still keep it on.
- Start over in a manga chapter's information sheet closes the sheet, as it does for episodes.
- For You cards a Save brought in say "Like one you saved" instead of "Like one you liked".
- Settings › Picture tags no longer counts a video or animation as tagged while its frames are still to be read.
- Going back to a For You trail in the grid returns to where you were in it, instead of its top.
- Series from extensions that need extra details to find a series or chapter again now open after you restart the app or import a Mihon or TachiyomiJ2K backup, instead of failing to load their chapters or pages.
- Tapping the Series tab again while on it no longer collapses its search bar.
- Opening a chapter of a series from sites like Asura no longer shows an HTTP 403 error while its pages load: the series cover shown meanwhile is fetched the way the site requires. The cover now also shows for the first chapter you open after starting the app, and for downloaded chapters.
- Opening an extension series chapter no longer fails some of its pages with "Network request failed unexpectedly" while the app checks for extension updates.
- In vertical reading, a long page that is still loading no longer looks like a blank black screen until you scroll to its middle: its spinner, or its Retry if it failed, stays in the part of the page you can see.
- Tapping a tab while the app was still sliding past it to another tab did nothing. It now goes to the tab you tapped.
- Undoing a save to a Codex you just created with New also removes that Codex, instead of leaving it behind empty.
- History was blank while it loaded. It now shows that it is loading.
- The offline storage error told you to reopen storage. It now has a Retry button.
- A Codex's tag list could show a post's raw ID. It no longer does.
- A backup preview said "1 profiles". Counts now use the right singular and plural.
- Holding a video for 2× sped it up a moment after the 2× label appeared, and released it a moment after the label left. Speed now changes with the label.
- Using the Back gesture from the edge of the full-screen Viewer often swiped to the next post on the way out, marking a post you never saw as watched. The Back gesture no longer changes posts.
- Tapping Retry on a Hitomi page while its gallery was still loading could shrink the gallery back to its cover when the retry finished. The full gallery now stays.
- Pixiv animations in the Viewer started over from the first frame when you swiped away and back. They now continue where they left off, as GIFs do.
- In a Hitomi gallery saved for offline, the Viewer's page overview loaded each page from the site instead of the saved copy, so its tiles stayed blank offline. They now show the saved pages.
- A creator page that failed to load while you were offline didn't say so, and stayed on its error after the connection came back. It now says you're offline and retries by itself, as Search and For You do.

- Resetting a search, tapping the Search tab again, or opening a post from a related shelf while Search was still returning to your saved place could be undone a moment later, when the list jumped back to the old place. The list now stays where you sent it.
- Coming back from a post to Search with the search bar open made the results slide down for a moment. They now appear in place.
- A creator's bio that you had expanded collapsed again after you opened one of their posts and came back. It now stays expanded.
- When Cloudflare challenged Iwara or nhentai, every request failed as if the site were down. They now switch to the site's mirror, as they do during other outages.
- Leaving a Hitomi search while another search was loading the same index could make the other search fail. It now finishes loading.
