Purpose / big picture

A sparse feed card must not erase resolved saved media. Multi-save commits the complete selection before optional caching. Collection tiles load counts and at most eight cover posts each, scoped to the active profile; tags load only for the selected action sheet. Local filters advance past hidden pages within a finite budget and offer Continue afterward. Recommendation attempts reuse one prepared model. Animation progress remains local to playback.

Context and orientation

A canonical Post is identified by source and provider ID. Room stores it once while Codex (saved collection), Likes and Recents hold independent memberships. Sparse means unresolved fields, not authoritative deletion. A route owner is a navigation-scoped ViewModel. Provider continuation tokens remain independent from local filtering.
