Network and image delivery

Branch perf/network-and-image-delivery, cut from origin/main at 76ff418a. Owner: Claude, for the developer.

Purpose / Big Picture

Searches and first loads feel slow. An audit found twelve causes across three areas: connections stall on a dead IPv6 path, the screen waits for work it does not need to wait for, and several sources download full-size images where a thumbnail would do. After this work a fresh app opening Search shows cards sooner, each card costs fewer bytes, and a broken IPv6 network no longer costs seconds. Someone can see it working by running scripts/live_latency_bench.py against two builds on a real phone.

Current Status

Validating. All findings and one found by measuring (A13) are committed. Results are in the before and after report. Next action: the developer reviews the branch; nothing has been pushed or merged.

Context And Orientation

Three HTTP stacks. Provider API calls go through core-sources/.../http/DefaultSourceHttpClient.kt and CustomSiteHttpClient.kt, both built on executeHttpRequest (HttpURLConnection). Images use Coil's OkHttp client from app/.../TheoriaApplication.kt. Video uses app/.../viewer/VideoHttpDataSource.kt. OkHttp is only present transitively at 4.12.

Happy Eyeballs means trying IPv6 and IPv4 at nearly the same time and using whichever connects first. OkHttp 5 does this by default; HttpURLConnection and OkHttp 4 try one address at a time.

Dual-stack host means a host that publishes both IPv4 and IPv6 addresses. On 2026-11-09 these were Danbooru, Hitomi, Iwara and rule34.xxx. Gelbooru, nhentai, Pixiv, Paheal, rule34video and rule34gen published no IPv6, which makes them natural controls.

Unified search is core-domain/.../orchestration/UnifiedSearchOrchestrator.kt; app/.../search/SearchViewModel.kt publishes its result. Card image choice is app/.../media/PostMedia.kt. Hitomi URLs are built in core-sources/.../hitomi/HitomiMediaUrlResolver.kt and HitomiPostMapper.kt.

Constraints from AGENTS.md: every visible animated or video card must keep autoplaying, so animated Hitomi galleries keep their animated preview. Protect com.theoriacodex. Do not screenshot or record provider feeds.

Surprises And Discoveries

  • The phone has public IPv6 and tries it first, but TCP over IPv6 timed out on all four dual-stack hosts tested. IPv4 connected in 60 to 120 ms.
  • Hitomi's webpbigtn is 640 px on its long edge, against originals up to 5600 px, which suits a card about 540 px wide.
  • Compose 1.12.1's staggered grid has no cache-window option, so preloading has to go through Coil.
  • Danbooru answered 503 (maintenance) and Iwara 403 (browser verification) on both builds, on HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/2. Those failures are the provider's; the fix only makes them fail in under a second instead of 20 to 30.
  • Gelbooru's untagged list took about 8 s with sort:id:desc and about 1.5 s without. A probe with a real key saw the same 40 posts in the same order, untagged and tagged. Tagged searches were fast either way.
  • The app reopens on its last tab, so the first version of the benchmark started its timer after a reopened feed had painted, and reported a false 25 s wait.
  • OkHttp rethrows anything but IOException on its dispatcher threads. A SecurityException from DNS killed the fixture process, where HttpURLConnection had returned it to the caller.
  • nhentai's Viewer images swing between about 1.5 s and 6 s on both builds: 2 of 11 baseline runs and 6 of 11 final runs hit the slow mode. The sample is too small to call a regression, and the slow mode exists without this work.
  • Paheal's filterless feed gets no card images on either build, and rule34gen's card images fail 12 of 12 on either build. Neither is caused by this work.
  • The nhentai swing is the provider's: its page hosts sometimes stop sending partway through a file. On 88 pages from the Mac, 2 stalled for about 18 s while a second request on a new connection answered in about 1 s. Time didn't follow file size (correlation -0.07) or protocol (HTTP/2 and HTTP/1.1 stalled alike). The hosts honour Range, so the app resumes a silent page after 2.5 s.
  • Paheal dropped data-ext for data-mime and its URLs have no extensions, so every file's type came out unknown, tagged searches included.
  • rule34gen's RSS thumbnails answer 404 while its listing page's work. Separately, on both rule34gen and rule34video, listing cards after the first four carry an inline placeholder in src and the real address in data-rd-src.
  • Follow-up validation on the AVD with isolated packages: the maintenance notice appeared against Danbooru's live 503 and stayed gone after Dismiss, and Test all sits in the Built-in header. The feed journey passed For You, then failed at Watched Recents with ten cards playing on software decoders. That step was never reached before, so a physical run must decide it. The warm-up, IPv6 learning and page rescue have not been measured on the phone.
  • The For You autoplay failure was the journey's. For You is built from sideways shelves, and the journey only scrolled vertically, so the fourth card of a row, the Ugoira, never came on screen.

Recovery

Each finding is its own commit, so one that misbehaves can be reverted alone. Benchmark runs append to their output directory; use a new --out to start clean. The benchmark restores the Debug app's applied query after each unified case. If a run stops halfway, the Debug app's last query may be the filterless default, which is harmless.
