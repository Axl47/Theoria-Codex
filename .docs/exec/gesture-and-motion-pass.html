Purpose / Big Picture

After this work the Viewer responds to fast flicks, keeps video playing through swipes, zooms where your fingers are, and closes with a pull-down or a back swipe that tracks the finger. Reordering, screen changes and Viewer controls animate instead of cutting. New gestures: hold for 2× speed, swipe up for info, pull-to-refresh, tab re-tap to top and drag-to-select.

Context And Orientation

Path under app/src/main/java/com/theoriacodex/app/Role
viewer/ViewerScreen.ktFullscreen route. Owns a post pager containing one media pager per post. Both have user scrolling disabled and are driven by hand.
viewer/ViewerMediaGestures.ktTap, double-tap and swipe recognition.
viewer/ViewerMediaPage.kt, viewer/ViewerTransformState.ktOne media page, its pinch handling and zoom math.
viewer/ViewerReturnTransition.ktCard-to-fullscreen geometry driven by one progress float (0 is fullscreen, 1 is the card).
ui/motion/ (new)Shared motion and haptic vocabulary.
ui/components/ReorderListState.kt, ui/components/ListReorderHandle.ktShared reorder drafts used by the Codex Library and For You.
ui/TheoriaApp.kt, ui/AppNavigation.ktNavHost transitions and the swipeable Home tab pager.

Constraints from AGENTS.md: every visible animated card autoplays simultaneously; likes stay local; no installs on com.theoriacodex. The feed behind the Viewer is not composed until the Viewer pops, which is why dismissal is phase 1 only.

Terms: settle is the animation after release. Catch is a finger landing during a settle. Commit is writing the selected post or media into Viewer state.

Phases And Work Plan

#PhaseObservable outcome
1Shared motion and haptics moduleDurations and springs come from TheoriaMotion; haptic meanings from TheoriaHaptics.
2Zoom fixesDouble-tap zooms anywhere on stills, at the tapped point, animated. Pinch follows the fingers. Pan stays on the image.

4Gesture columns and dismissal35/30/35 columns. Pull-down and predictive back shrink the media, then hand off to the card return. Swipe up opens info.
5Hold for 2×, like pop, hapticsHolding video plays at 2× until release. Long-press info stays on other media.
6Navigation transitionsCodex and creator screens no longer use the 700 ms default crossfade.
7Reorder remakeNeighbours glide, drops settle, lists scroll at their edges, each step ticks.
8Hard cutsViewer controls, Settings pages and For You views transition.
9Pull-to-refresh and tab barSearch and For You refresh by pull. Re-tap scrolls to top. Tab taps slide.
10Drag-to-select and Codex morphPaint selection in Codex edit. Library tile grows into its Codex.

Each phase is one Conventional Commit with its CHANGELOG.md entry. Out of scope by owner decision: hold-to-peek, and the possible post-close tap dead zone, which still needs a device check.

Surprises And Discoveries

  • 2026-11-03: Viewer page layers are disposed as they leave the viewport, which would kill a gesture caught mid-settle. The recognizer must live above the pagers.
