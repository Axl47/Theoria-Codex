Purpose / Big Picture

After this work the Viewer responds to fast flicks, keeps video playing through swipes, zooms where your fingers are, and closes with a pull-down or a back swipe that tracks the finger. Reordering, screen changes and Viewer controls animate instead of cutting. New gestures: hold for 2× speed, swipe up for info, pull-to-refresh, tab re-tap to top and drag-to-select.

Constraints from AGENTS.md: every visible animated card autoplays simultaneously; likes stay local; no installs on com.theoriacodex. The feed behind the Viewer is not composed until the Viewer pops, which is why dismissal is phase 1 only.

Terms: settle is the animation after release. Catch is a finger landing during a settle. Commit is writing the selected post or media into Viewer state.

Surprises And Discoveries

  • 2026-11-03: Viewer page layers are disposed as they leave the viewport, which would kill a gesture caught mid-settle. The recognizer must live above the pagers.
  • 2026-11-03: GsonWireContractTest has two failures (stale LegacySettingsStoreRecord manifest) that also fail on 0ccd6e31, before this work. Left alone as out of scope.
  • 2026-11-03: Owner reported a blank frame on tap and a full-height flash on opening and swiping. A frame-by-frame pixel probe cleared the return transition, whose geometry paints correctly. It reproduced two renderer defects: the preview left as the full image began a crossfade from transparent (center pixel ff002600 over black), and a differently shaped preview painted beyond its media. ViewerFirstFramesTest failed on both before the fix. Whether these are everything the owner saw needs the Samsung.
  • 2026-11-03: Owner reported a blank frame leaving a Codex. CodexDestinationStateBoundary resubscribed with null initial values each time the Library re-entered composition and drew nothing until the database answered, leaving the morph no tile to land on. CodexLibraryViewModel keeps the last values on the Home entry; only a cold start still waits, which preserves the scroll-anchor guard. Fading both screens also showed the backdrop, so the screen underneath now stays opaque.
  • 2026-11-03: Owner reported the fallback return on on-screen Recents posts. The default All view is a column of rows that never joined the return system, so its cards reported a null grid and recordBounds rejected them. It now finds the row by post, because viewing a post can regroup its rows.
  • 2026-11-03: Codex detail could not tell "not answered" from "deleted", and browsingMetadataOwner was created per navigation entry, so every Viewer and Codex waited on fresh queries. The owner is now activity-scoped and rememberRetained keeps each entry's last values, replacing CodexLibraryViewModel.
  • 2026-11-03: Device evidence corrected three wrong host-side theories. Slow-swipe cancels were on the main tabs, whose pager kept its 50% default; Viewer swipes committed in real-finger logs. The blank frame on tapping a post was feed video cards releasing their players, recorded as empty tiles for about 7 frames. Likes took 290-460 ms to answer against 110 ms for a 125-post Codex, outlasting the 320 ms fade.
  • 2026-11-03: LocalPostWatchPresentation was a static composition local holding a value that changes on every post open, recomposing the whole app mid-animation. A main-thread stack sampler showed idle in 12 of 276 samples during the tap, and 99 or more after the fix.
  • 2026-11-03: Real-finger logs showed rapid flicks dropped when a caught settle's leftover offset made them look like flicking back.
  • 2026-11-03: The Codex detail screen has no cover header, so the morph targets the whole screen rather than a header.

Outcomes And Retrospective

All phases and audit findings A1-A9 are implemented. Tab commit points, feed cards on tap, Likes opening and the open animation were exercised on the Samsung with the debug package only; the production package was not touched. Debug builds run slower than release, so open-animation smoothness still needs a release-like measurement. Feel, haptics and predictive back remain for the owner.

Follow-up open-cost measurement on the Samsung (debug build, warm opens of a nine-video Codex): tap to first Viewer frame is about 140ms, the first composition frame is 41-59ms, and two frames of roughly 75-145ms follow landing. The first tears down the screen underneath, including ExoPlayer blocking on each feed surface detach; the second composes chrome, timeline and strip together. Removing PlayerView's unused controller, deferring system media controls until landing, and moving reading-position writes off the main thread reduced the work inside the animation. The first composition and the two post-landing frames remain.
