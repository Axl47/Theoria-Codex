Purpose and context

The Creator page currently toggles Likes without routing the committed outcome to a shelf owner. A shelf is a transient related-post row inserted after a liked post. Search and For You already share RelatedPostsRouteController for request cancellation, 15-second timeout, retry, history and promotion (moving a liked recommendation into the owning feed).

Relevant files: app-logic/src/main/kotlin/com/theoriacodex/app/creator/state/CreatorStateContract.kt; app/src/main/java/com/theoriacodex/app/creator/CreatorProfileViewModel.kt and CreatorProfileScreen.kt; app/src/main/java/com/theoriacodex/app/ui/routes/CreatorRoute.kt; app/src/main/java/com/theoriacodex/app/ui/TheoriaApp.kt. The shared controller, presentation and projected grid remain authoritative.

Phases and work plan

  1. Add transient related state and typed Creator actions, a static RELATED Viewer effect, and controller wiring in CreatorProfileViewModel. Clear on refresh, creator replacement and capability loss. Preserve upload continuation during promotion.
  2. Inject the application loader through CreatorRoute. Include shelf posts in duration ownership. Render shared RelatedPostsShelf through TwoColumnProjectedPostStaggeredGrid; map paging demand to canonical indices and open upload cards by visible index.
  3. Deliver committed Like outcomes through an exact captured Creator owner/query identity, including retained Viewer callbacks. Validate lifecycle and UI behavior.

Loading, empty, failure/retry, previous/next and dismiss states use the existing shared components. Unsupported providers stay idle. No new persistence or provider mutation.
