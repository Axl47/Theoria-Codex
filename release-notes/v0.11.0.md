# Theoria Codex v0.11.0

## Highlights

- Keep complete Codex collections available offline and back up your library across profiles.
- Return to the exact gallery page, save named searches, and discover more from liked posts and followed creators.

## New

- Offline collections with storage management, download progress, cancellation, and Retry.
- Profile backup export and previewed additive restore, with optional history and reading positions.
- Named saved searches that preserve grouped tags, Multi-Search, and For You recipes.
- Local creator follows, manual new-post checks, and a Followed feed with source and creator filters.
- Related-post shelves after liking, with previous and next shelf navigation.
- Video quality choices, picture-in-picture, headset controls, and Loop, Play once, or Next in queue playback.

## Improvements

- Faster local tag suggestions and earlier Pixiv animation playback while frames load.
- Remember the last gallery page independently from highest reading progress and restore the exact Viewer post order.
- Adapt navigation, grids, and Viewer information to wider windows.
- Keep collection Retry and Undo available across navigation and Activity recreation.
- Remove Viewer OCR and translation controls.

## Fixes

- Retry a failed provider without losing successful results or repeating search history.
- Preserve original media metadata when saving or reopening offline videos.
- Save complete galleries with distinct Unicode filenames.
- Prevent expired Pixiv requests from replacing a reconnected account.
- Keep submitted Search tags cleared when focus returns, including Android 8.
- Stop animated media when only its card caption remains visible.
