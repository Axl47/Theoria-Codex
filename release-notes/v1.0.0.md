# Theoria Codex v1.0.0

## Highlights

- Find your way around a more consistent Theoria, with redesigned Search, filters, Saved, For You, Recents, and Settings.
- Explore your activity on a dedicated Stats screen with trends and your top Codices and posts.
- Build searches more comfortably with a full-screen tag editor and clearer filter controls.

## New

- Explore Stats on their own screen, including time spent browsing and watching, activity trends, and ranked Codices with their top posts.
- View recent activity trends across 7-day, 30-day, and yearly periods. Theoria keeps up to 400 days of local activity history while preserving existing lifetime totals.
- Filter Search by additional date ranges or choose custom dates.
- Jump directly to interests in For You, and browse Recents as a day-by-day timeline of posts and searches.
- See total storage usage in Settings, with a breakdown of offline content, cache, and downloads.

## Improvements

- Edit searches in a full-screen view with all tags visible and clear options for required, alternative, and excluded tags.
- See active filters at a glance and clear them together with Undo. Filters now use a consistent layout across Search, For You, Codices, and creators.
- Open Likes, Followed, and Searches directly from their covers in the Codex library.
- Find Codex and post actions in labeled menus, with automatic tag rules in their own Auto-save sheet.
- Rerun a saved search directly from the search editor.
- See your current settings at a glance and open each option in its own sheet or page.
- Keep private thumbnails for posts in Stats top lists after clearing the cache or removing a like.
- Use **Save** and **Saved** consistently across Recents and For You.
- Start loading and buffering the next post while swiping in the fullscreen viewer, with a thumbnail shown during the transition.

## Fixes

- Try another available video decoder when the preferred decoder cannot start.
- Play the Codex exit animation when using Android’s Back button or gesture, including from Saved sections.
- Correct the direction of Codex screen transitions when going back.

## Changes

- The minimum score filter has been removed. Older saved searches continue to work and ignore any saved score filter.
