# Theoria Codex v0.6.4

## Highlights

- A focused reliability release for Recents and saved Codex content.

## New

- Recents now has separate Codex and Watched filters with independent clear actions.
- Reopening a Codex entry preserves its saved-collection context.

## Improvements

- Refreshed saved posts replace stale thumbnails and update Codex covers together.
- Saved collections are safer during app updates.
- The app starts faster and runs more efficiently.

## Fixes

- Viewer identifies deleted posts with “Post was deleted” instead of a generic playback failure.
- Fixed Recents entries reopening under the wrong history section.
