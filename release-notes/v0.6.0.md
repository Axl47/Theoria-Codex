# Theoria Codex v0.6.0

## Highlights

- Added Hitomi as a complete source across Search, Viewer, Creators, Codex, deep links, and recommendations.
- Expanded Viewer with a unified Media Overview for still images, animated media, Ugoira, and video.
- Added animated WebP playback across every supported Android version.

## New

- Search Hitomi broadly or narrow results by tags, artists, characters, series, groups, types, and languages.
- Discover available Hitomi types and languages before entering a search.
- Browse Hitomi creator profiles and share or copy direct creator links.
- Open supported Hitomi gallery, reader, and artist links directly in Theoria Codex.
- Filter For You recommendations to a single source or keep everything together in a unified feed.
- Control animated WebP playback with play, pause, restart, and live frame progress.

## Improvements

- Hitomi results open immediately in Viewer while the complete gallery loads in the background.
- Media Overview preserves the original order of mixed-media posts and animates visible WebP pages, making similar pages easier to identify.
- Detailed searches keep the selected category—such as artist, character, or series—when reopened, saved to Codex, exported, or restored after restarting the app.
- Hitomi galleries preserve their ordered pages, animated images, and playable anime videos.
- Large videos stream normally instead of being downloaded in the background.
- Source checks now cover real searches, galleries, creators, and media with clearer failure information.
- Unified source settings are cleaner and no longer show inactive health information.

## Fixes

- Repaired legacy Gelbooru media links and added lasting recovery for saved posts with broken previews.
- Prevented background media loading failures from crashing Viewer.
- Fixed Hitomi startup and search failures that could occur specifically on Android devices.
- Improved recovery when Hitomi changes its media servers.
- Fixed Pixiv searches failing when optional artwork information is missing.
- Corrected tag discovery for Iwara, NHentai, and Rule34 video sources.
- Removed the unwanted background behind the transparent Hitomi logo.
