# Theoria Codex v2.0.0

## Highlights

- A new Series tab reads manga and watches anime from Mihon and Aniyomi extensions: follow series, get notified of new chapters and episodes, download chapters, and bring your library over from a Mihon, TachiyomiJ2K or Anikku backup.
- For You is now a deck: swipe right to like, left to skip, up to save to a Codex, and down to hide a tag.
- Trails: follow a post's creator, similar posts or tags from the Viewer, and step back along the way you came.
- Saving is quicker: the Viewer's Save button suggests the right Codex, and holding any post lets you drop it on a Codex.
- Plus more than 100 bug fixes and performance improvements.

## New

- Series: browse and search extension sources, read chapters left to right, right to left or vertically, and play episodes with server, subtitle and audio choices. Extensions install from the repos Mihon and Aniyomi use, and each stays off until you turn it on.
- Series keeps followed series checked for new chapters and episodes, in the background or when you pull down, shows unread counts on the covers, and offers Switch source when a site falls behind, keeping what you've read.
- Swipe opens any feed or Codex one post at a time, and Codex Swipe and Clean review make tidying a Codex quick.
- Library holds Codices, Saved (Likes, Followed, Searches) and History. Followed creators and saved searches are checked for new posts, and new posts show a dot until you open them.
- Galleries can be read as one vertical scroll, chosen per gallery.
- A saved search can be edited in Search, and the search box also finds your Codices, followed series, creators, settings and switches.
- Codex offline status shows as a ring, a pill in Library shows downloads in progress, and downloads keep going after you close the app with Back.

## Improvements

- The bottom bar is now Search, For You, Library and Series. Settings opens from the gear in Library and Series.
- The Viewer's actions moved to a dock at the bottom. Rotation follows how you hold the phone, with a rotate button and a lock that stays on between visits.
- Search updates as you edit it, in a sheet over the results.
- Removing things no longer asks first where it can be undone: Undo appears where the item was.
- In Play Next and slideshows, each post stays at least five seconds, and swiping back pauses moving on until you go forward.
- Scrolling, swiping through posts, and opening the app and the Viewer are faster.

## Fixes

- Returning to Search lands on the exact place you left, including after Undo search or reopening the app.
- When a site answers "too many requests", the app backs off for 10 minutes instead of retrying into a longer block.
- The screen can turn off again while a video is paused and while you browse feeds, and stays on while you read a chapter.
- The Back gesture in the Viewer no longer swipes to the next post on the way out.

## Known Issues

- Episodes can't be downloaded yet, extensions that stream by torrent can't be turned on, and anime split into seasons isn't supported yet.
- Tapping to seek in a short looping video can briefly show its first frame.
- If you had Animated only on, tap it once more after updating.
- A backup with statistics made by this version may need this version or newer to restore.
