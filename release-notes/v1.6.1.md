# Theoria Codex v1.6.1

## Highlights

- Landscape is roomier: the search tray under a post's tags folds to one line, and the Viewer's thumbnail strip fills the width.
- The Viewer's rotate button is always there, so you can turn the screen by hand on any post.

## Improvements

- The search tray under a post's tags, and in Codex tag search, folds to one line beside Search. It starts folded in landscape; tap it to edit the search.
- The Viewer's post strip shows as many thumbnails as fit instead of seven widely spaced ones.

## Fixes

- The rotate button no longer floats high above a landscape video's timeline or a multi-page post's page bar.
