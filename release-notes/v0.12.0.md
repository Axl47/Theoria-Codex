# Theoria Codex v0.12.0

## Highlights

- Discover personalized interests and manage searches in For You.
- Navigate and control fullscreen media with new gestures, thumbnails, and related-post previews.
- Browse saved content together and combine saved searches into a feed.
- Manage downloads, revisit searches, and see more detailed Codex statistics.
- Enjoy more reliable playback, search restoration, and download recovery.

## For You

- Browse personalized interest sections and an Explore section.
- Pin sections, dismiss interests, and shuffle suggestions while keeping your pins.
- Add, reorder, and dismiss searches directly from the overview.
- Move a For You search to Saved Searches with **Save to Codex**.

## Fullscreen viewer

- Open a **More like this** thumbnail strip after liking a post and jump to related posts without leaving the viewer.
- Navigate posts using a scrollable thumbnail strip below the seek bar.
- Preview video and animation timelines while scrubbing, then seek when you release. Enable **Legacy Instant Seek** if you prefer seeking continuously while dragging.
- Swipe up or down along the sides of a video to adjust brightness or media volume.
- Automatically enter picture-in-picture when leaving the viewer during video playback.

## Saved and Codex

- Browse Likes, Followed, and Saved Searches together in the new Saved overview.
- Like posts directly from Searches and Followed.
- Combine selected Saved Searches into a live feed.
- Clean a Codex by removing posts already present in selected collections.
- See browsing time, watching time, post opens, and top posts for individual Codices.

## Search and downloads

- Revisit previous queries and source selections with **Search Undo**.
- Browse downloads, open saved files, remove history entries, and delete downloaded files individually or in bulk.

## Improvements

- Search takes up less space while browsing, with a compact toolbar and simplified source selection. The previous search bar remains available in Legacy settings.
- Fullscreen media expands from its feed card, follows your gestures, and returns to its place in the feed when closed.
- Resume recently viewed media and see watched progress across more feeds. Nearby videos also retain their playback positions while you browse within the viewer.
- Remember your viewer play mode, adjust playback speed with a slider, and see a thin progress bar while fullscreen controls are hidden.
- Find Codex destinations more easily with search, relevant suggestions, and indicators for collections that already contain a post.
- Filter Recents by source and view storage usage by category.
- Browse nhentai artist profiles and galleries directly from posts.

## Fixes

- Improved video playback recovery, including an automatic retry for certain playback failures.
- Reduced delays when opening or switching animated posts, and show cached previews and the first Pixiv animation frame sooner.
- Keep videos and animations playing while dragging the seek bar without resuming media you paused.
- Restore video and exported animation thumbnails from downloaded files after reopening the app.
- Improve recovery of interrupted downloads, animation exports, and offline saves after an app restart.
- Keep successful search results visible when another source fails during pagination.
- Restore search positions more reliably when returning to results or using Search Undo.
- Preserve library scroll position when returning from a collection.
- Keep related-post navigation and the original feed order intact when browsing recommendations from fullscreen.
- Keep liked posts visible until their related-post recommendations are dismissed, including when hide filters are enabled.
- Resume autoplaying feed videos at the position reached in fullscreen.
- Open nhentai links at the linked gallery page, even when the page loads after the cover.
- Improve tag counts and suggestions, including counts supplied by nhentai and counts for typed terms.
- Preserve For You history when clearing ordinary searches.
- Keep Like and preference changes across navigation, with Retry feedback when needed.
- Fix the Gelbooru Animated filter to match additional animation and video tags.
- Prevent shuffled For You interests and Explore from failing with a sorting error.

## Compatibility

- **Android 16 or newer is required.** This release supports Android 16 and Android 17.
