# Theoria Codex v1.3.0

## Highlights

- Share a provider link from any app to open it in Theoria.
- Back up automatically every day to a folder you choose. Restoring merges into what you already have instead of duplicating it.
- Save tags and whole "or" groups straight from the search editor, and give groups a name.
- Feeds recover on their own when your connection comes back.

## New

- Settings > Sources has a shortcut that lets provider links open in Theoria directly, and shows which sites are on.
- Daily backups under Settings > Storage & app. Theoria keeps the last seven and tells you if one fails. Backups now include your statistics when you include Recents.
- Undo right after deleting a Codex or clearing Likes.
- Hold a post and choose More like this to open its related posts without liking it. This works in Search, For You, Recents, creator pages, and the Followed and Searches collections.
- Liking a post in Recents or in the Followed and Searches collections now opens its related posts, as it does elsewhere.
- Search editor: tap a tag and choose Save tag to save it, or to remove it if it's already saved. Save an "or" group from any of its tags' menus, and name it when you save it or later from Manage.

## Improvements

- Search editor: your saved tags appear in the Saved section with a star. Tap one to add it, again to exclude it, and once more to clear it. A saved "or" group adds or removes the whole group.
- Saved tags that match what you type appear first in the suggestions.
- Manage saved tags from the Manage action in the Saved section. The Favorite tags sheet is now called Saved tags.
- Feeds now say you're offline instead of blaming each source when the connection is the problem.
- On a metered network, feed cards autoplay a smaller video stream when the source offers one.

## Fixes

- Restoring a backup adds what's missing to your existing profiles and collections, keeps profile names, and no longer creates duplicates when you restore the same backup again.
- Retrying a search that timed out repeats that search instead of the one before it.
- For You shows sources that need an account and opens Source accounts from them, as Search does.
- Opening a video on a metered network or with Data saver no longer downloads a larger version in advance that the Viewer then doesn't play.
- Search Reset is available immediately for restored tags.
