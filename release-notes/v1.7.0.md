# Theoria Codex v1.7.0

## Highlights

- Booru posts can open the artist's original post, or find the same file on your other booru sites.
- A file that several sites host shows once in search, and a like, save or watch on one site carries over to its copies elsewhere.
- Lock the app behind your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
- Play a slideshow through the Viewer's queue.

## New

- "Open original post", in the Viewer's More menu and the long-press menu on feeds, goes to the page the booru says the file came from, usually the artist's Pixiv or X post. It opens in the Viewer when that site is one of your sources, and in the browser otherwise.
- "Find on other sites", in the same menus, looks the file up on your other Danbooru, AIBooru, Gelbooru, Rule34.xxx and custom booru sites and opens what it finds, one post per site.
- App lock, in Settings under Storage & app, asks to unlock when you open the app and when you return after more than a minute away, and hides the app's screen in recent apps.
- A slideshow button on still images in the Viewer shows each image for five seconds, plays videos and animations through once, and moves through the queue until it ends or you touch the screen.
- Save to Codex turns green with a checked bookmark when the post is already in one of your Codices, in the Viewer's Info and the long-press menu on feeds.

## Improvements

- A file hosted on several booru sites shows as one card when you search them together, in Search, For You and live Codices.
- A post you liked, saved or watched on one booru site is recognised on other sites showing the same file: its heart is filled, the Hide liked, saved and watched filters hide it, and For You stops suggesting it. Unliking it removes the like everywhere. Your Library still lists only the posts you saved.
- Swiping to the next or previous post shows its comment count right away.
- Swiping between videos with the controls showing keeps a timeline on screen the whole way.
- With the controls hidden, the thin progress line runs along the bottom of the media instead of the screen.
- Save to Codex lists the Codices that already hold the post first, in either sort order.
- Save to Codex no longer offers Saved or Likes; liking a post is how it gets into Likes.
- Save to Codex uses the full width in landscape, with Codices in two columns.
- The long-press menu on feeds and the Viewer's Info offer the same actions: feeds gain Like, Share and "Open in browser", and the Viewer's More menu gains "More like this", "Start over", "Copy tags", "Copy link", and "Remove from Codex" when opened from a Codex.
- Share opens Android's share sheet with the post's link instead of copying it.

## Fixes

- Restoring a backup with "Also apply global preferences" now restores the Library's sort order and the old search bar choice.
- When a site wants a browser check, its search error and source chip offer "Verify in browser" instead of a Retry that would fail again.
- Holding a video at 2x no longer changes brightness or volume when your finger slides up or down.
- Sliding your finger after a long press opened Post Info no longer drags the Viewer behind it.
- The Viewer's rotate button glides with the bottom bar instead of jumping when the controls hide or return.
- Removing a single post from a Codex says "1 post removed" instead of "1 posts removed".
