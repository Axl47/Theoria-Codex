# Theoria Codex v1.4.0

## Highlights

- Add your own booru sites, search Danbooru and AIBooru, and manage every source from one Sites page.
- Search is quicker: Unified results appear as soon as the first sources answer, and reopening the app shows your last search right away.
- Multi-page posts are easier to browse, and videos start, swipe and seek faster.
- Offline collections now stay available, and copies you already saved are recovered.

## New

- Add a site in Settings > Sites by typing its address. Theoria checks whether it is a Danbooru or Gelbooru 0.2 site and adds it as a source you can search, like and save from.
- Sites that need an API key ask for one. Keys stay encrypted on your device and are never included in backups.
- Sites behind a browser challenge, including built-in sources like Iwara, can be verified in the app. Once Theoria gets through, the page closes and the source works right away.
- Test all in Settings > Sites checks every source with a real search and marks the result on its row.
- Drag the page bar on a multi-page post to move page by page, with a floating preview of the target page.
- Multi-page posts show stacked cards in the Viewer's post strip, and the open post unfolds its pages there.
- For You learns across Pixiv, Danbooru, AIBooru, Gelbooru and Rule34.xxx, so Likes on one source suggest the same interests on others.
- Share a post link from an added site to open it in Theoria. Importing a Codex with posts from a site you haven't added offers to set it up.
- Search now has saved searches alongside saved tags. Save the search on screen and run it again with a tap.
- Hold a tag in any post's tag sheet to save or unsave it.
- Like a post from the Viewer's info sheet.

## Improvements

- Accounts and Sites are now one page, listing built-in and added sources with each one's account and browser check.
- Source menus mark sites that are down or need verification. Unified search tells you when a site is missing from its results, with Verify in browser when that helps.
- The first search after opening the app starts faster on Wi-Fi.
- Feeds start loading upcoming card images before you reach them on unmetered networks.
- Hitomi and Iwara cards load small thumbnails instead of full-size images, and Gelbooru's untagged feed loads several times faster.
- Gallery pages fill in with small previews and sharpen once they stay on screen. Rule34.xxx posts show a lighter sample before the original.
- Videos start sooner and resume faster after a seek, and scrub previews appear sooner.
- Neighbouring videos preload only their opening seconds, which saves data on every network.
- The next gallery page is ready before you reach it, and images already on your device appear straight away.
- Saving offline reuses what's already on your device instead of downloading it again.
- Post cards show their details as plain marks over soft shading. Partly viewed galleries show a progress line.
- Recents groups each day's searches above the posts you viewed.
- Pixiv tags show their English name beside the Japanese tag.
- Scrolling, liking and typing in Search are smoother, and large Codex libraries open faster.

## Fixes

- Collections saved for offline use now stay available, and copies saved before this update are recovered when the app starts.
- On networks with broken IPv6, sources no longer stall for up to 30 seconds or fail to connect.
- When a site is down, searches fail quickly instead of waiting about a minute, and a brief network hiccup no longer marks every source as missing.
- R34V videos play again in feeds and the Viewer.
- Paheal, Rule34Gen and Rule34Video cards show their thumbnails again.
- nhentai pages that stall partway through recover in a few seconds instead of hanging for up to half a minute.
- Switching source in Search shows that source's last search.
- Gelbooru and Rule34.xxx tags with apostrophes or ampersands display correctly and find their posts.
- Pixiv searches keep tags containing parentheses, and typing a tag's English name searches its Japanese tag.
- A failed full image in the Viewer falls back to its preview instead of spinning, and galleries keep their cover on screen until the first page loads.
