# Theoria Codex v1.9.1

## Highlights

- Dismiss a feed's loading error and keep browsing the posts already loaded.
- Vertical and square videos fill the picture-in-picture window.

## Improvements

- When the next page of a feed fails to load, its error card has Dismiss next to Retry, so it stops taking up the top of the screen. This covers Search, account pages, and Saved searches and Followed in the Codex, plus Search's "Source account required" notice. A new, different error shows again; errors with no posts to browse still offer only Retry.
- Account pages support pull to refresh, which also resumes loading after a dismissed error.

## Fixes

- Vertical and square videos fill the picture-in-picture window instead of showing black bars on the sides and top.
