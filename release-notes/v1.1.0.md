# Theoria Codex v1.1.0

## Highlights

- A redesigned Viewer puts the artwork first, with floating controls and colors drawn from the art.
- Swiping, zooming and closing posts now follow your finger and feel much more responsive.
- Duration filters keep posts coming as you scroll, without reshuffling the feed.
- Posts, Codices and large collections open faster, without blank screens.

## New

- Pull down on the Viewer to close it, or swipe up for post information.
- Hold a video, GIF or ugoira to play it at 2× until you let go.
- Sort the Codex Library by name, date added or item count; switching back to Manual restores your own arrangement. Add to Codex uses the same sort for its non-suggested Codices.
- Long-press and drag in a Codex's edit mode to select a range of posts.
- Pull down to refresh Search and For You, or tap the current tab again to scroll to the top.
- Rename a recommendation profile from its Settings menu, keeping its likes, Codices and hidden tags.
- Animated posts show a Play icon in the Viewer's thumbnail strip and recommendation drawer.

## Improvements

- Viewer: the seek bar uses the full width in portrait and thickens while you drag. Scrub previews are sharper and show the time and how far you've moved.
- Viewer: brightness and volume appear on a rail beside your swipe, and double-tap seeking no longer opens the controls.
- Viewer: multi-page posts show a segmented page bar, and the playback speed appears beside the time when it isn't 1×.
- Viewer: new recommendations add to the drawer instead of replacing it.
- Gestures: a short flick changes post, a slow drag commits at about 16% of the screen, and flicking back cancels. Video keeps playing while you swipe.
- Gestures: on video, brightness and volume use the outer columns of the screen, leaving the centre for closing and information. Double-tap and pinch zoom around your touch.
- Duration filters: posts appear in feed order, and slow ones join the bottom of the list. Lookups use far less data and run several at a time.
- Duration filters: Pixiv ugoira now appear instead of always being hidden.
- Library and feeds: Codices appear as cover tiles matching Saved collections, and their actions now open by long press. For You and Recents use Search's source menu, and watched posts are marked with an eye icon.
- Motion: creators, Codices, tabs, Settings pages and For You views now animate between screens. Reordering glides, ticks and scrolls long lists at their edges.

## Fixes

- Feeds no longer reshuffle into different columns after you close the Viewer or scroll back up.
- Zoomed media stays on screen, and gallery pages of different shapes can pan to their edges.
- Browsing many Codices no longer keeps growing memory use.
- Large Codices open with their posts showing, and Codices and the Library no longer flash blank or empty.
- The Viewer no longer dips to black when the full image replaces its preview, or drops rapid flicks.
- Feed videos stay visible behind an opening Viewer, and closing is smoother because they wait until the post lands.
- Posts return to their cards in Recents' All view.
- Gelbooru videos no longer vanish from duration filters for five minutes when the host is briefly overloaded.
- Searching with a Codex's tags now always runs that search.
- The navy band behind the status bar above the Viewer is gone.
